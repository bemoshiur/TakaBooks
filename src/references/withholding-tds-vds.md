## Scope this file will cover
- Who is a withholding entity, for income tax and for VAT separately.
- The TDS rate matrix by payment type, with the section that authorises each deduction.
- VDS rates by service, and which supplies are outside VDS.
- Deposit mechanics: treasury challan, economic code, and the deposit deadline.
- Certificates — the income tax deduction certificate, and Mushak 6.6 for VDS.
- The two sides: deducted *from* the user (a receivable) vs deducted *by* the user (a liability
  plus a deposit duty plus a certificate to issue). Never conflate them.
- Consequence of deducting and not depositing: the liability stands and the underlying expense is
  exposed to disallowance — the same failure costs twice.
