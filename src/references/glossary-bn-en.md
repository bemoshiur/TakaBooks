# Glossary — বাংলা ↔ English

**Current as of:** 2026-09-06
**Sources:** term usage on the NBR portal (https://nbr.gov.bd) and the Bangla Mushak / আয়কর
forms it publishes; the Bangla and English titles of the Acts listed in the last section;
Bangladeshi professional accounting usage. This file cites no figure and needs none.
**Status:** POPULATED — term pairs only. Terms printed on NBR forms (মূসক, উৎসে কর কর্তন,
করবর্ষ, চালানপত্র, দাখিলপত্র …) are as NBR prints them; ordinary accounting and payroll terms
are common professional usage and await word-by-word review by a Bangladeshi CA / ITP. Contains
no rate, threshold, deadline or section number — those live in `data/rates-AY<year>.toml` and
the subject references.

**How to use.** Users write Bangla, Banglish or English; the middle column is the spelling you
will see typed. To find a form or notice on the NBR portal, search with the Bangla term. Pair
the Bangla and English on first use in a reply: মূসক / VAT, উৎসে কর কর্তন / TDS.
