# Glossary — বাংলা ↔ English

**Current as of:** 2026-09-06
**Assessment year context:** AY 2026-27 (income year 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026). Rates,
thresholds and deadlines live in `data/rates-AY<year>.toml` and the subject references — never
here.
**Status:** POPULATED — terminology and statutory identifiers, sourced as described below.

**What this file contains.**
1. **Term pairs** — Bangla ↔ Banglish ↔ English, so a reply can meet the user in the language
   they typed and so a term can be searched on the NBR portal in the right script.
2. **Mushak form numbers with their Bangla titles** (§ *Mushak form numbers*), and the rule of
   the VAT & SD Rules 2016 each one is anchored in. A glossary that cannot name a form is
   useless, so these are kept — each with its sourcing marked.
3. **Statutory short titles with their years**, and the Act or SRO number behind each
   (§ *Statutes*), because "the Finance Act" without a year is not an identifier.

**What this file does NOT contain.** No tax rate, no monetary amount, no registration or
exemption threshold, and no filing deadline. If a figure is wanted, route to the subject
reference; if the subject reference is still a STUB, say so and do not improvise.

**Sourcing.** Everything marked below is traceable to the TakaBooks research pack —
`docs/research/bd-tax-research-2026-09-05.md` (the section number is cited) and
`docs/research/paripatra-2026-27-rates-excerpt.txt` (NBR, *আয়কর পরিপত্র ২০২৬-২০২৭*,
02 September 2026). Three markers are used, and **only** in the *Mushak form numbers* and
*Statutes* tables and in the "Sourcing notes" under a term table:

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **✔** | Attested in the research pack at the section cited, which read it from the gazette or the primary Act/Rules text. |
| **⚠** | Listed in that document's **UNVERIFIED / CONFLICTING** section. Usable as terminology; **not** settled as law. Verify before citing it in anything a return is filed from. |
| *(unmarked)* | Ordinary Bangladeshi professional and NBR-portal usage. Not quoted from a primary text in the research pack, and awaiting word-by-word review by a Bangladeshi CA / ITP. |

Two limits on the Bangla spellings, stated plainly:
- The **Bangla titles of the Mushak forms** are, per research **§E15**, renderings decoded from
  rule headings and the ICAB manual. They should be checked against the form schedule in the
  amended Rules before being printed on anything.
- The **Paripatra 2026-27 PDF's text layer uses a legacy Bangla font encoding** that does not
  extract cleanly (research §"Confidence and how sources were used"). It confirms *concepts*
  and figures, but no Bangla spelling in this file was taken from it verbatim.

> **Not professional advice.** This glossary is a translation aid inside a bookkeeping tool. It
> is not tax, legal or accounting advice, it creates no adviser relationship, and it must not be
> relied on for filing a return, answering a notice, or any other dealing with the NBR, the RJSC
> or a court. Bangladeshi tax law changes every year and mid-year by SRO. Before you act, check
> the current text at https://nbr.gov.bd and https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd and take advice from a
> licensed Income Tax Practitioner, Chartered Accountant or VAT consultant. Where this file says
> something is unverified, treat it as unverified. **Absent beats wrong.**

**How to use.** Users write Bangla, Banglish or English; the middle column is the spelling you
will see typed. To find a form or notice on the NBR portal, search with the Bangla term. Pair
the Bangla and English on first use in a reply: মূসক / VAT, উৎসে কর কর্তন / TDS.

**Sourcing notes**
- **কর দিবস is no longer the operative term.** Research §1.9 records that *"Tax Day" (কর দিবস)
  has been replaced* by **রিটার্ন দাখিলের নির্দিষ্ট তারিখ** — *specified date for filing return* —
  inserted as **ITA 2023 s.2(80A)** by Act 89 of 2026 §29(ট), read from bdlaws. **✔** Keep কর
  দিবস in the glossary because users and older guides still type it; answer in the new term.
- **অগ্রিম কর / চূড়ান্ত কর / টার্নওভার ট্যাক্স** are the three limbs of the marginal note of
  **ITA 2023 s.163** as substituted by the Finance Act 2026 — *"অগ্রিম কর, চূড়ান্ত কর এবং
  টার্নওভার ট্যাক্স"* (research §2.15, quoted in clean Unicode). **✔** Note that the VAT-side
  advance tax at import is **আগাম কর**, a different term — see the VAT section.
- **সারচার্জ** was split by the Finance Act 2026 into a wealth surcharge and a new
  **environmental surcharge** (research §1.4, ITA 2023 ss.2(14), 2(21), 2(86Kha)). **✔** for the
  split; the Bangla পরিবেশ সারচার্জ is professional usage, not quoted from the gazette.
- **IT-10B / IT-10BB.** Research §1.9(e) names IT-10B the *Statement of Assets and Liabilities*
  and IT-10BB the *Statement of Lifestyle Expenses*. **✔** for the English and the codes. The
  Bangla title shown for IT-10B (which includes ব্যয়) is professional usage and has not been
  checked against the current NBR form.
- **পিএসআর / PSR** — Proof of Submission of Return, **ITA 2023 s.264** (research §1.13). **✔**
  for the term and the section. ⚠ The *list of services* that require a PSR could not be
  obtained as amended by the Finance Act 2026 — do not quote a list from here or anywhere else
  without checking s.264.
- **জুলাই যোদ্ধা** is given in clean Bangla at research §1.2 as a taxpayer category new relative
  to pre-2025 law. **✔** for the term; the threshold attached to it is not in this file.
- **এ-চালান.** Research §2.16 quotes the requirement that deposits be made *"এ-চালানের
  মাধ্যমে"*. **✔**
- **নির্দিষ্ট ব্যক্তি / specified person** is used throughout research §2 (e.g. §2.6, §2.10) as
  the ITA's withholding-entity concept. **✔** for the English; the Bangla is professional usage.

**Sourcing notes**
- **আগাম কর, not অগ্রিম কর.** Research §3.5 renders the VAT-side advance tax at import as
  **আগাম কর** (Mushak 4.1, *আগাম কর ফেরত/সমন্বয়*, Rule 19). **✔** An earlier version of this
  glossary used *অগ্রিম কর* here; that is the income-tax term (see the income tax section) and
  the two must not be swapped.
- **নিবন্ধনসীমা / তালিকাভুক্তিসীমা** are quoted in clean Bangla at research §3.2 for the VAT
  registration and turnover-tax enlistment thresholds (VAT & SD Act 2012 ss.2(57) and 2(48)).
  **✔** for the terms. ⚠ The *figures* are the most commonly misreported numbers in Bangladesh
  VAT (research §3.2, §E9) — they are not in this file; get them from the VAT reference.
- **মূসক চালানপত্র** is quoted verbatim from the VDS SRO at research §3.10. **✔**
- **আংশিক উপকরণ কর রেয়াত** is quoted at research §3.6 for the apportionment rule where a person
  makes both taxable and exempt supplies. **✔**
- **উৎসে মূল্য সংযোজন কর কর্তন / VDS** — the operative rules are the *উৎসে মূল্য সংযোজন কর কর্তন
  ও আদায় বিধিমালা, ২০২৫*, SRO 182-Ain/2025/310-Mushak (research §3.10). **✔** See the Statutes
  table for the trap around a non-existent SRO number that circulates for these rules.
- **ইভ্যাট / eVAT** is the system named in VAT & SD Act 2012 s.10 as replaced by the Finance Act
  2026 (research §3.3) and in the amended Rules 4 and 8 (research §3.5). **✔** for the English;
  the Bangla transliteration is common usage.
- ⚠ **Return frequency is not a term but is worth knowing when you translate দাখিলপত্র:** VAT
  returns are **quarterly** from 1 July 2026 (research §3.7). Any material calling the দাখিলপত্র
  a monthly return is out of date. No dates are given here — see the VAT reference.
