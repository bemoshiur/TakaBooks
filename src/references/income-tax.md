## Scope this file will cover
- Who must file a return, which return form applies, and to which tax circle/zone.
- Personal slabs and the tax-free threshold (করমুক্ত সীমা) by taxpayer category.
- Investment rebate / বিনিয়োগ রেয়াত — eligible investments and how the rebate is computed.
- Minimum tax (ন্যূনতম কর) and surcharge (সারচার্জ) — when each bites.
- Corporate rates by entity type, listing status and sector.
- Allowable vs disallowed expenditure, including the banking-channel conditions on payments.
- Tax depreciation — a separate schedule from accounting depreciation (অবচয়).
- Advance income tax / অগ্রিম আয়কর (AIT) and its set-off against assessed tax.
- Assessment (কর নির্ধারণ), audit selection and the appeal route.
