## Scope this file will cover
- Registration (BIN) vs turnover-tax enlistment, and the turnover figures that decide which.
- Standard, reduced and zero rates; exemptions (অব্যাহতি) and the SRO that grants each.
- Supplementary duty / সম্পূরক শুল্ক, kept in its own account and its own return line.
- Input tax rebate / উপকরণ কর রেয়াত — the conditions that create it and the defects that kill it.
- The Mushak 6.x record set: 6.1 ক্রয় হিসাব পুস্তক · 6.2 বিক্রয় হিসাব পুস্তক · 6.2.1 combined ·
  6.3 চালানপত্র · 6.4 · 6.5 · 6.6 VDS certificate · 6.7 credit note · 6.8 debit note · 6.9 · 6.10.
- Mushak 9.1 return (দাখিলপত্র) and Mushak 4.3 input-output coefficient (উপকরণ-উৎপাদ সহগ).
- The ledger-to-register-to-return tie-outs that `vat.py` must enforce, and what to do on a break.
- Column layout of each register, verified against the current gazette form — not a secondary copy.
