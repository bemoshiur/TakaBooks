## Scope this file will cover
- Salary heads and perquisites: what is taxable, what is exempt, and the ceilings that apply.
- TDS on salary (উৎসে কর কর্তন — বেতন) and the employer's statements and certificates.
- Provident fund / ভবিষ্য তহবিল — recognised vs unrecognised, and the accounting consequence.
- Gratuity / আনুতোষিক — approved funds, accrual, and deductibility.
- WPPF / শ্রমিক অংশগ্রহণ তহবিল and the workers' welfare funds under the Labour Act 2006:
  applicability test, the share of profit, the split across funds, and the funding period.
- Festival bonus (উৎসব বোনাস), overtime and leave encashment treatment.
- Employer contributions vs employee deductions, and why the two must not be netted.
