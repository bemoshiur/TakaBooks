## Scope this file will cover
- Income tax: late filing, late payment, failure to file, and the interest basis.
- VAT: late or non-submission of the return, non-issue of চালানপত্র, unrecorded supply.
- Failure to deduct at source, and failure to deposit what was deducted.
- Non-maintenance or non-production of prescribed records and registers.
- How interest is computed — the base, the period and the rate source.
- Appeal, waiver and settlement routes, and who may represent the taxpayer.
- Deliberate framing: penalty exposure is stated as a range with its source, never as a threat
  and never as an invented figure.

## Definition of done
1. Every figure carries a primary-source URL and a "current as of" date.

   file and is flagged as unverified here.
