> **Not professional advice.** Penalty exposure is stated here as the statute states it — as a
> ceiling, a range, or a formula, with the discretion the statute gives the officer. It is not a
> prediction of what will be imposed. Verify with a licensed **আয়কর আইনজীবী / Income Tax
> Practitioner (ITP)** or **চার্টার্ড অ্যাকাউন্ট্যান্ট / Chartered Accountant (CA)**.

## How to read this file

- **"Up to Tk X" means a ceiling, not a charge.** Most income tax penalties are discretionary
  ("আরোপ করিতে পারিবেন" — *may impose*). Where the statute is mandatory ("আরোপ করিবেন" — *shall
  impose*), the line says so.
- **No penalty without a hearing** in income tax — **ITA s.280**. In VAT the same rule applies
  under **s.85(4)** with one exception: a late-return penalty needs no hearing.
- **Penalty, interest and the tax itself are separate and cumulative.** VAT Act s.85(5) and s.127(4)
  say so expressly.
- Amounts are Bangladeshi taka. **লাখ** = 100,000; **কোটি** = 10,000,000.

## 1. Income tax — filing late, or not at all

### 1.1 Additional tax built into the filing window — ITA s.170

Not a penalty in the statutory sense: it is an extra charge attached to the date of filing, imposed
by the return provision itself. **s.170**, wholly substituted by the **Finance Act 2026 s.97**,
effective 1 July 2026 — http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52000.html

| Who | Filed | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Individual / HUF | 1 Jul – 30 Sep | **Incentive**, not a charge: 5% of tax payable under s.173(2) **or Tk 25,000, whichever is LESS** |
| Individual / HUF | 1 Oct – 31 Dec | Nothing either way |
| Individual / HUF | 1 Jan – 31 Mar | **2% of tax payable or Tk 3,000, whichever is HIGHER** |
| Individual / HUF | 1 Apr – 30 Jun | **5% of tax payable or Tk 5,000, whichever is HIGHER** |
| Company / other entity | 2 months before the deadline (15 Jan 2027 for a 15 Mar 2027 deadline — file early, the statute does not settle the boundary day) | **Incentive**: 5% **or Tk 25,000, whichever is LESS** |
| Company / other entity | Final 2 months to the deadline | Nothing either way |
| Company / other entity | After the deadline, inside the assessment year | **2% of tax payable or Tk 25,000, whichever is HIGHER** |

> **The entity minimum really is Tk 25,000 against Tk 3,000 for an individual.** Both figures were
> read from the primary Bangla text of the same section. The asymmetry is startling and it is real.

### 1.2 বিলম্ব রিটার্ন / Delayed return — ITA s.174

Once the assessment year itself has passed, s.170 no longer applies and **s.174** does — substituted
by the **Finance Act 2026 s.102**, effective 1 July 2026 —
http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52004.html

| Situation | Additional tax |
|---|---|
| Filed **suo motu** (স্বপ্রণোদিতভাবে) after the s.170 time expires | Tax under s.173, **plus 10% of the tax payable or Tk 5,000, whichever is HIGHER** |
| Filed after the tax year has passed, in response to a notice under s.212(1) in a s.212(4)(ka) case | Tax under s.173, **plus 15% of the tax payable or Tk 10,000, whichever is HIGHER** |

**s.174(4):** in a delayed return, exempt income is taxed as if the exemption did not apply — except
income under Sixth Schedule Part 1 clauses (1), (4), (5), (6), (7), (8), (17) and (35), and exempt
income counted in computing income from employment. **Filing late can therefore cost the exemption
itself, not just the additional tax.** This is the most under-appreciated consequence on this page.

This replaces the old 2%-per-month (maximum 24 months) charge under the pre-2026 text.

### 1.3 Failure to file — ITA s.266(1)

http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52183.html — failure, without reasonable cause, to file
a return under **ss.166, 172, 191, 193 or 212**. The **উপ কর কমিশনার / Deputy Commissioner of Taxes
(DCT)** *may* impose:

- **10% of the tax charged on the person's last assessed income**, subject to a **minimum of
  Tk 1,000**; **plus Tk 50 for every day** the default continues.
- **Ceiling** — the penalty may not exceed:
  - **Tk 5,000** for an individual taxpayer **never previously assessed**;
  - for an individual **previously assessed**, **50% of the tax payable on the last assessed income,
    or Tk 1,000, whichever is HIGHER**.

### 1.4 Failure to pay — ITA ss.271, 275, 269

| Default | Exposure | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Failure to pay the **স্বীকৃত করদায় / admitted tax liability** | Up to **25% of the whole tax**, or, as the case may be, an amount equal to the unpaid part | **s.271** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52188.html) |
| Default in payment of tax (assessee in default) | Up to **the amount of the unpaid tax**, in addition to the arrear itself | **s.275** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52219.html) |
| Failure to pay **অগ্রিম কর / advance tax** under s.154, or filing an estimate under s.155 known or believed to be untrue | Up to **the difference between the tax payable and the tax actually paid** | **s.269** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52186.html) |

### 1.5 Interest on an advance-tax shortfall — ITA s.162

http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51992.html

Where advance tax paid **plus** tax withheld under Part 7 comes to **less than 75%** of the tax
determined on regular assessment, the taxpayer pays **simple interest at 10% per annum** on the
difference between the total paid and 75% of the assessed tax.

**Proviso, substituted by the Finance Act 2026 s.89:** where a **delayed return is filed after the
end of the assessment year relating to the income year**, the interest rate is **50% higher** — i.e.
**15% per annum**. Before that substitution the uplift was triggered by filing after the specified
date; it is now the end of the assessment year, which is a real narrowing.

**Period (s.162(2)):** from **1 July** of the assessment year to the date of regular assessment, or
**two years** from 1 July, whichever is shorter. Interest is reduced pro rata if the underlying
amount is reduced on appeal, revision or reference (s.162(4)).

## 2. Income tax — উৎসে কর কর্তন / withholding failures

This is where the largest exposure sits for an ordinary SME, because three separate provisions stack.

### 2.1 The core charge — ITA s.143

http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51973.html

A person who fails to deduct or collect, deducts at a lower rate or amount, fails to deposit or
deposits less, **or fails in any other compliance** under Part 7 is a **খেলাপি করদাতা / defaulting
taxpayer** (s.143(1)), and is liable for:

| Limb | Amount |
|---|---|
| s.143(2)(ka) | The amount not deducted or collected |
| s.143(2)(kha) | The shortfall between what should have been and what was deducted or collected |
| s.143(2)(ga) | The amount deducted but not deposited |
| s.143(2)(gha) | Any other Part 7 non-compliance — **up to Tk 1,000,000 (Tk 10 lakh)** |
| **s.143(3)** | **An additional 2% per month** on the s.143(2)(ka)–(ga) amounts |
| **s.143(4)** | That 2% runs from the date fixed for deduction or collection to the date of deposit — **capped at 24 months** |

**Who is liable (s.143(6)–(7)):** for government bodies, projects, programmes and units, the
individuals responsible for approving the payment or issuing the clearance, registration, licence or
permit. For everyone else, the entity itself **and** the individuals responsible for approving the
payment. **s.143(5):** a reasonable opportunity of hearing must be given first. **s.143(8):** no
recovery where the payee has already paid everything due under s.143(2) and (3).

### 2.2 The 150% charge on the payer — ITA s.56, s.41(6), s.56A

**s.56**, wholly substituted by the **Finance Act 2026 s.49**, effective 1 July 2026 —
http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51886.html

> **s.56(1)(ka):** where the Part 7 withholding provisions are not complied with, **the amount
> short-deducted, short-collected or short-paid PLUS a further 50% of it** is payable — i.e. **150%
> of the tax defaulted**.
> **s.56(1)(kha):** where tax paid through s.143 proceedings comes to less than that, the shortfall
> **plus 50% of it** is payable.

The same rule applies to agricultural income (**s.41(6)**) and, new under the Finance Act 2026, to
withholding on the **acquisition of capital assets** for a business or profession (**s.56A / ৫৬ক**).

**Worked example from NBR's own Paripatra 2026-27:** applicable TDS Tk 400,000, actually deducted
Tk 200,000, shortfall Tk 200,000 → payable = 200,000 + (200,000 × 50%) = **Tk 300,000**.

### 2.3 What the Finance Act 2026 REMOVED

**ITA s.55(ka) — the disallowance of the underlying expenditure on a TDS default — was DELETED** by
the Finance Act 2026 s.48(kha), effective 1 July 2026
(http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51885.html, amendment footnote 3). Guidance written
before July 2026 stacks a disallowance on top of the s.56 charge. **It no longer applies.** Under-
stating exposure is as wrong as overstating it: the s.56 150% charge is the operative sanction now.

**s.55(o) still bites separately**, and for a different reason: any sum paid to a person who is
liable to furnish **রিটার্ন দাখিলের প্রমাণ / Proof of Submission of Return (PSR)** under s.264(3)
clauses 24, 26, 27, 33, 36 and 37, and who fails to furnish it at the time of payment, is
**disallowed as a business expense**. Clause 24 covers salary and allowances paid in a management,
administrative or production-supervisory position — so salary paid to a manager without a PSR is
disallowed in the employer's assessment.

### 2.4 Certificates and returns — ITA ss.144, 266(2)

| Default | Exposure | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Issuing a certificate of deduction, collection or payment **without** actual deduction, collection or payment | The issuer is **personally liable** for the amount | **s.144** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-51974.html) |
| Failure to file the **s.177 quarterly withholding return**, or to submit or present information under it | **10% of the tax charged on last assessed income, or Tk 5,000, whichever is HIGHER**, plus **Tk 1,000 for each month or part** the default continues | **s.266(2)(ka)** |
| Failure to furnish the **s.145 deduction certificate** | Up to **Tk 5,000**, plus **Tk 1,000 per month or part** continuing | **s.266(2)(kha)** |
| Failure to furnish information required under s.200 | Up to **Tk 50,000**, plus **Tk 500 per day** continuing | **s.266(2)(ga)** |
| Obstructing a withholding verification under s.147 | Up to **Tk 5,000,000 (Tk 50 lakh)** | s.147(2) |

A withholding verification may cover only the relevant financial year and **at most the two
preceding financial years** (s.147(4), new under the Finance Act 2026).

| Default | Exposure | Section |
|---|---|---|
| **Failure to maintain accounts** in the prescribed manner (s.72(3)), no income from rent of tangible property | Up to **1.5× the tax payable**; **Tk 5,000** where total income is within the tax-free limit | **s.267(1)** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52184.html) |
| Same, with income from rent of tangible property | **50% of the tax payable on that rental income, or Tk 5,000, whichever is HIGHER** | **s.267(2)** |
| Using a **fake TIN, or another person's TIN**, in a return or any document requiring one | Up to **Tk 20,000** | **s.268** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52185.html) |
| **আয় গোপন / Concealment of income** — misstating income, assets, liabilities, expenditure or any other material particular so as to evade an amount payable | **Penalty = evaded amount × 10% × N**, where N = the number of years from the assessment year in which the evasion occurred to the assessment year in which it was discovered, **inclusive**. The authority **shall** impose it. | **s.272** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52189.html) |
| **False or non-conforming audit report by a Chartered Accountant** — not certified as following IAS/IFRS and ISA, or untrue or incorrect | **Not less than Tk 50,000 and not more than Tk 200,000**, on the CA. | **Filing a fake audit report** — one not signed by a Chartered Accountant, or credibly untrue | **Tk 100,000** for that income year, on the taxpayer | **s.274** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52218.html) |
| Non-compliance with a notice under **ss.167, 168, 179, 181, 183 or 212** | Up to **the tax chargeable on total income** | **s.270** — [link](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429/section-52187.html) |

> ### ⚠️ The s.272 concealment formula changed — most published tables are wrong
>
> The current text imposes a **single limb**: `A = evaded amount × 10% × N`.
> The previous text imposed **A + B**, where `A = evaded amount × 15%` and
> `B = evaded amount × 10% × N`. That older two-limb formula was **replaced by the Finance
> (FY2025-26) Act 2026 (Act 89 of 2026) s.123** — see amendment footnote 1 on the bdlaws page. Any
> table still showing "15% plus 10% per year" is quoting the superseded version and **overstates**
> the charge. Read the section before quoting a number to a client.
>
> Note also that N is measured **inclusive of the year of discovery** ("সেই করবর্ষসহ"), so a
> concealment discovered in the same assessment year gives N = 1, not 0.

NBR has publicly warned (August 2025) that filing a **"zero return"** when not genuinely below the
threshold is prosecutable concealment under ss.312–313.

Framework rules in the same section:

- **s.85(2):** any other failure to do what the Act or Rules require, or doing what they forbid, is
  penalised in the prescribed manner and amount, graded by seriousness and repetition.
- **s.85(2ka):** **no penalty** where the shortfall, non-payment, refund, excess credit or wrong
  adjustment arose from a mistake or misinterpretation and the final assessed tax **with interest**
  is subsequently paid.
- **s.85(3):** a criminal case and a penalty proceeding may both run on the same facts.
- **s.85(4):** a hearing must precede a penalty — **except** for row (চ), late filing.
- **s.85(4ka):** no late-filing penalty for the tax periods during which a business was temporarily
  shut for want of supplies and later restarted.
- **s.85(5):** penalty is payable **in addition to** VAT, supplementary duty, turnover tax, interest
  and any fine.

### 5.2 Interest — VAT Act s.127

http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1106/section-42422.html

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Tax not paid by the due date, **or a rebate or decreasing adjustment taken irregularly** | **1% per month, simple**, from the day after the due date to the day of payment |
| VDS arrears | **2% per half-year, simple** |
| **Maximum period** | **24 months** — the Explanation defines "until the day of payment" as running from the day after the due date, including any pending period, but **not more than 24 months** |

The words "or irregularly takes a rebate or decreasing adjustment" were inserted by **Act 89 of 2026
s.25**. Interest paid on an amount later shown not to have been payable is refunded (s.127(3)), and
interest is **in addition to** any fine or penalty (s.127(4)).

### 5.3 Appeal pre-deposits — slashed by the Finance Act 2026

All on the **disputed tax, excluding penalty**, payable at the time of filing the appeal.

| Forum | From 1 July 2026 | Previously | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **কমিশনার (আপীল) / Commissioner (Appeals)** | **1%** | 10% | [s.121(2)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1106/section-42416.html) |
| **আপিল ট্রাইব্যুনাল / Appellate Tribunal** | **1%** | 10% | [s.122(2)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1106/section-42417.html) |
| **High Court Division** | **2%** | 10% | [s.124(4)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1106/section-42419.html) |

An appeal to the Commissioner (Appeals) is filed within 90 days; the Commissioner may allow a further
60 days for reasonable cause (s.121(2) proviso).

### 5.4 Practical enforcement beyond the penalty

- **BIN lock.** On a late VAT return the VAT authority issues a notice; if the business is still
  non-compliant after **21 days** an assessment order is issued and NBR may **temporarily lock the
  BIN**, suspending import and export activity. The BIN is unlocked within about two days of the
  return being filed. *(Source: KPMG Bangladesh, Bangladesh Tax 2026, p.70 — a professional summary,
  not the statute. Treat as practice, not law.)*
- **Late filing permission does not buy time to pay.** The Commissioner may permit a late return on
  application, but that permission **cannot extend the actual tax payment date by more than one month
  and does not alter the liability to interest** — s.65,
  http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1106/section-42361.html
- **An amended return cannot recover a missed rebate.** Rule 49(1A) of the VAT and SD Rules 2016 bars
  it, so an expired input-tax-credit window cannot be cured by amendment.
- **The legacy-settlement window (s.137A) closes 31 December 2026** — see
  `compliance-calendar.md` §7. It caps interest at 24 months; it does not waive the tax.

| Failure to prepare and file the **annual list of members / Schedule X** (s.36) | Up to **Tk 200 for every day** the contravention continues, on the company **and** every officer who knowingly and wilfully authorises or permits it | [s.36(5)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-32860.html) |
| Failure to file the **balance sheet and profit and loss account** with the Registrar (s.190) | Up to **Tk 100 for every day** the failure continues, on the company and every officer knowingly and wilfully responsible | [s.190(3)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-33579.html) |
| A director failing to take all reasonable steps to comply with s.183 (accounts laid at the AGM, and their 9-month staleness limit) | Up to **Tk 5,000 for each such offence** | [s.183(5)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-33572.html) |
| Issuing, circulating or publishing a balance sheet **not signed**, or not accompanied by the profit and loss account, auditor's report and directors' report | Imprisonment up to **6 months**, or fine up to **Tk 2,000**, or both, on every officer responsible | [s.189(4)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-33578.html) |
| **Failure to hold the AGM** (s.81(1)) or to comply with a court direction under s.81(2) | Up to **Tk 10,000** each on the company and every officer in default, **plus up to Tk 250 for every day** after the first day of continuing default | [s.82](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-32906.html) |
| Failure to file particulars of, or changes in, directors, manager or managing agent (**Form XII**, 14 days) | **Tk 500 for each breach**, on the company and every officer knowingly and wilfully responsible | [s.115(4)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-32939.html) |
| Carrying on business without complying with the registered-office requirements (**Form VI**, 28 days) | Up to **Tk 200 for every day** of non-compliance | [s.77(4)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-32901.html) |
| Failure to notify the Government within 7 days that its s.210(4) power to appoint an auditor has become exercisable | Up to **Tk 1,000** on the company and every officer in default | [s.210(5)](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-33854.html) |
| **Foreign company** failing to comply with any provision of Part XI | Up to **Tk 5,000** on the company, **plus up to Tk 500 for every day** after the first day of continuing default; the same on every officer or representative knowingly and wilfully responsible | [s.384](http://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-788/section-34666.html) |

These are **per-day** fines on a **continuing** default, and they run on the company **and** on
individual officers. A company that has not filed for several years carries a compound exposure that
is easy to underestimate.

### 6.2 RJSC administrative filing fees — distinct from the fines above

Read from RJSC's own published fee page (Bangla), content last updated 26 July 2023 —

https://app.roc.gov.bd/psp/fee_calculator
Governing chain: S.R.O. 136-Ain/2016 → **S.R.O. 101-Ain/2019** (which introduced the per-year late
fee) → Ministry of Commerce Notification of 9 July 2023, effective 1 July 2023, which raised the
company per-document fee to Tk 200.
