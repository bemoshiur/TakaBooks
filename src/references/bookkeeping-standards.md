## Scope this file will cover
- Books of account required by the Companies Act 1994, and by whom.
- The Financial Reporting Act 2015 and the Financial Reporting Council's role.
- IFRS / IFRS for SMEs as adopted in Bangladesh (BFRS / BAS) — which entity applies which.
- Which entities must be audited (নিরীক্ষা), by whom, and on what timetable.
- Financial statement formats and the disclosures a Bangladeshi SME set must carry.
- Record retention periods, for both the income tax and the VAT regimes.
- RJSC filing obligations and the annual return.
- Cash-basis reality vs accrual requirement — and why the year-end must be a report, not a
  reconstruction.
